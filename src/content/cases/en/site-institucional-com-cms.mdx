### Who it is for

The website serves the brand and its visitors, who find company and product content there. The content management system (CMS) serves whoever looks after the website's content.

### How it works

Company and product content is kept in the CMS, built with WordPress. The dynamic templates are page layouts: instead of storing the text in the page itself, they pull that content from the CMS and display it on the website. The homepage is the first page visitors see when they enter the official website.

### What was built

- The homepage of the brand's official website.
- Several dynamic templates that display company and product content.
- The brand's content management system (CMS), built with WordPress.

### The technology, in plain words

- **PHP**: a programming language made to run on servers and widely used in websites and web systems; WordPress is written in it, and it was used in the project alongside WordPress.
- **WordPress**: an open-source content management system (CMS) used to create and manage websites; it was the base of the brand's CMS.
