### Who it is for

The system was designed for a multinational manufacturer.

### How it works

Static electricity is charge that builds up on people and objects; a discharge is what you feel in the small shock from touching a doorknob. A static detection system starts from sensing that charge in order to prevent damage to the boards.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Python**: a general-purpose programming language, easy to read and widely used for automation, data analysis and web systems; it was one of the two technologies in the base structure.
- **JavaScript**: the programming language of the web, which gives pages behavior in the browser and also runs on servers; it was the other technology in the base structure.
