### Who it is for

The app is just for the brand's store owners and distributors. Each of them uses the app to manage their own sales and deliveries. End consumers do not use this app: they buy through the brand's main app.

### How it works

The reseller app and the brand's main app are part of the same ecosystem. The main app works as a marketplace: there, consumers buy household gas (LPG, liquefied petroleum gas) and get it delivered. The reseller app is the side of those who sell and deliver: in it, store owners and distributors manage their sales and deliveries.

### What was built

- An Android app just for the brand's store owners and distributors.
- Sales management for each reseller, inside the app.
- Delivery management for each reseller, inside the app.
- The app's integration into the ecosystem of the brand's main app, the household gas marketplace.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets. The reseller app was built for it.
- **Java**: a general-purpose programming language, for many years the main language for building Android apps. It was one of the two languages used in the app.
- **Kotlin**: a programming language compatible with Java, officially supported by Google for building Android apps. It was the other language used in the app.
