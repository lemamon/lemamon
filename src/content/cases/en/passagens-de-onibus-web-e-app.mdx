### Who it is for

The system is for people who need to buy a bus ticket. Passengers can make the purchase in the browser or in the phone app.

### How it works

The system has three parts. The web application is the way to buy in the browser. The mobile app is the way to buy on a phone. The back-end server is the part that runs behind the scenes of the system, out of the passenger's view.

### What was built

- The web application, to buy bus tickets in the browser.
- The Android mobile app, to buy tickets on a phone.
- The back-end server, the part of the system that runs behind the scenes.

### The technology, in plain words

- **PHP**: a programming language made to run on servers and widely used in websites and web systems. It was one of the project's technologies.
- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets. The ticket purchasing app was built for it.
- **JavaScript**: the programming language of the web, which gives pages behavior in the browser and also runs on servers. It was one of the project's technologies.
