### Who it's for

For people who use the Micro:bit board together with an Android device. The project was created for a large education company.

### How it works

On the cable path, the board is physically connected to the device; on the wireless one, the two exchange data with no physical connection.

Besides the app, the project defined the architecture of this communication, that is, how the parts are organized to talk over both paths.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets. It is the platform of the project's app.
- **Java**: a general-purpose programming language, for many years the main language for building Android apps. It was used to develop the app.
- **C++**: a programming language common in software that works close to the hardware, such as that of boards and electronic devices. It was part of the project's development.
- **Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE)**: a version of Bluetooth designed to exchange small amounts of data while using little power. It is the wireless path between the board and the Android device.
- **Micro:bit**: a small programmable board created to teach programming, with buttons, LED lights and sensors. It is the board the app connects to Android.
