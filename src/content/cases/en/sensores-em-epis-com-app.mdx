### Who it is for

The system was built for a large energy company and concerns the workers who use wearable personal protective equipment (PPE), the safety gear they wear on the body while working. It is these workers' use of the equipment that the system monitors and validates.

### How it works

The sensors are built into the wearable PPE itself, so they sit on the equipment the worker uses. These sensors connect to a mobile app. Through this link between the sensors on the PPE and the app, the system monitors how the equipment is used and validates that workers use it correctly.

### What was built

- A set of sensors built into wearable PPE, the part of the system that sits on the protective equipment itself.
- A mobile app, for Android, that connects to those sensors and serves to monitor and validate correct PPE use.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Node.js**: a runtime that runs JavaScript outside the browser, widely used to build servers and APIs (the doorways through which one system hands data to another).
- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets; in this project, it is the platform of the mobile app.
- **C**: a general-purpose programming language, widely used for software that runs close to the hardware, such as in electronic devices and embedded systems.
