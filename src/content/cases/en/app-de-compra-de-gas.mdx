### Who it is for

The app was built for consumers who buy household gas (LPG) and want it delivered straight to them, and for the distributor, as its brand's main app.

### How it works

LPG is liquefied petroleum gas, the cooking gas used in homes. The app works as a marketplace, that is, an online sales space that brings together those who sell and those who buy, focused on selling this gas. From their phone, consumers buy the gas and get it delivered straight to them.

The app was built in two native versions, that is, each one made specifically for its system: one for Android and one for iOS. Each version was written in its platform's own language.

### What was built

- The Android app, built with Java and Kotlin.
- The iOS app, built with Swift.
- In both versions, the marketplace for selling and delivering household gas (LPG) straight to consumers.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets; in this project, one of the app's two platforms.
- **iOS**: Apple's operating system for the iPhone; in this project, the app's other platform.
- **Java**: a general-purpose programming language, for many years the main language for building Android apps; in this project, used in the Android version.
- **Kotlin**: a programming language compatible with Java, officially supported by Google for building Android apps; in this project, used in the Android version alongside Java.
- **Swift**: a programming language created by Apple, used for iPhone and iPad apps; in this project, it is the language of the iOS version.
