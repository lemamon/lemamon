### Who it is for

The app is for people who use wearable devices and want to follow their own health and wellness on their phones. That user is the one who sees their health routine in the app.

### How it works

It works in three steps. First, the app connects to the wearable devices. Next, it collects biometric metrics from those devices, that is, body measurements captured by the devices. Finally, it presents users with their health routine based on those metrics.

### What was built

- The mobile app for health and wellness monitoring.
- The app's connection to wearable devices.
- The collection of biometric metrics from those devices.
- The presentation of the user's health routine, based on the collected metrics.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Ionic**: a framework, that is, a ready-made code base, for building mobile apps with web technologies such as HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The app was built with it.
- **TypeScript**: a language created by Microsoft that adds types to JavaScript, which helps catch errors in the code before it runs. It is the language the app was written in.
