### Who it's for

The website was made for a food brand, during the pre-launch phase, and for anyone who visited the page in that period.

### How it works

With the sprite technique, the code shows the image frames of a movement one at a time, in sequence; the quick switch between them creates the illusion of animation.

### The technology, in plain words

- **HTML**: the language that organizes the content of a web page, such as text, images and buttons; it is the base of the website.
- **JavaScript**: the programming language of the web, which gives pages behavior in the browser and also runs on servers; it was used with HTML to build the website and its interactive animations.
