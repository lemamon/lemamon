### Who it is for

The app was made for those who need to monitor and measure vehicle flow on public roads. The project was a partnership with a public university.

### How it works

The app runs on Android and is based on computer vision concepts, the field of computing in which software analyzes images and recognizes what appears in them. The image analysis uses the OpenCV library, and the app applies that analysis for one purpose: to monitor and measure vehicle flow on public roads.

### What was built

- The Android app to monitor and measure vehicle flow on public roads.
- The app's computer vision part, built with the OpenCV library.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets; it is the platform the app runs on.
- **OpenCV**: an open-source library with ready-made computer vision functions to analyze images and video; it is the base of the app's image analysis.
