### Who it's for

The game is for people who want to play on their phones with others, in multiplayer matches. It was part of an initiative of a multinational company.

### How it works

One player creates the local room, and anyone connected to the same network joins it; the match takes place among those players.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Unity 3D**: a game engine, a tool for creating 2D and 3D games for phones, computers and consoles; it was the base for developing the game.
- **C#**: a programming language from Microsoft, used to program games in Unity; the game's logic was written in it.
