### Who it is for

The app is for tourists and visitors to a city: they are the ones the app guides and assists.

### How it works

Visitors use the app on an Android phone. The app plays two roles: guiding tourists around the city and assisting them during their visit. Because it is native, it was built with Android's own tools, not with web technologies adapted for the phone.

### What was built

- A native Android mobile app.
- The guide and support role for tourists and visitors in the city, inside the app.

### The technology, in plain words

- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets. The app was built natively for it, that is, with Android's own tools.
