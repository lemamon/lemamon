### Who it is for

The project was made for a court, part of the judiciary. The homepage is the entry point to the court's main portal and serves anyone who visits the portal. The redesign also considered the people who look after institutional news, since one of its goals was to make managing and publishing that news easier.

### How it works

The redesign changed two layers of the homepage. On the visual layer, there was a rebranding: a renewal of the page's visual identity. On the structural layer, the page's organization was rebuilt. The redesign served two goals: modernize the main portal's interface and make managing and publishing institutional news easier.

### What was built

- The new look of the main portal homepage, with the rebranding.
- The new structure of the main portal homepage, with the page's organization rebuilt.

### The technology, in plain words

- **PHP**: a programming language made to run on servers and widely used in websites and web systems; it was used to build the new homepage.
- **HTML**: the language that organizes the content of a web page, such as text, images and buttons; it is the base of the new homepage's structure.
- **CSS**: the language that defines how a web page looks, such as colors, fonts and spacing; it was used in the visual part of the redesign.
