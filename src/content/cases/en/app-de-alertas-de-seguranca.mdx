### Who it is for

The system involves two audiences. On one side, the local police, who need to share news, alerts and safety notices. On the other, the public, for whom that information is meant.

### How it works

The system has two parts: a mobile app and a server. The app is the part that lives on the phone; the server is the part that runs behind it, off the phone. Together, they serve one goal: getting news, alerts and safety notices from the local police to the public quickly.

### What was built

- The Android mobile app, the part of the system that lives on the phone.
- The server, the part of the system that runs behind the app.

### The technology, in plain words

- **PHP**: a programming language made to run on servers and widely used in websites and web systems. In this project, it is the server technology.
- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets. The system's mobile app was built for it.
