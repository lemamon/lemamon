### Who it is for

The system involves two audiences: the local police, who publish the information, and the public, who receive it.

### How it works

The app is the part that lives on the phone; the server runs behind it, off the phone.

### The technology, in plain words

- **PHP**: a programming language made to run on servers and widely used in websites and web systems. In this project, it is the server technology.
- **Android**: Google's operating system for phones and tablets. The system's mobile app was built for it.
